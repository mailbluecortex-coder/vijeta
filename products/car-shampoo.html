Vijeta Car Shampoo

Vijeta Car Shampoo • Snow Foam & Premium Vehicle Care

Engineered specifically for detailing studios, automated service centers, and auto enthusiasts. Generates clingy, blanket-like snow suds that soften grime for a touchless or gentle contact wash.

Formula Attributes

  • ✓ Hyper-Concentrated Snow Foam Generator
  • ✓ pH-Neutral: Won't Strip Waxes or Protective Coatings
  • ✓ Compatible with Pressure Washers & Manual Buckets
  • ✓ Gloss-Enhancing Polymers for Showroom Sheen
Application Methods

Foam Cannon & Manual Bucket Options

Foam Cannon Setup

Pour 100 ml of Vijeta Car Shampoo into a 1-Litre foam cannon reservoir and top off with warm water. Spray over the entire vehicle body. Let dwell for 4-5 minutes before pressure rinsing.

Hand Bucket Wash

Add 30-50 ml of shampoo to a 10-Litre wash bucket. Blast with a strong stream of water to churn up rich suds. Glide your microfiber mitt over vehicle panels with minimal downward pressure.
