Vijeta Car Wash Liquid

Vijeta Car Wash • Vehicle Cleansing & Streak-Free Shine

Engineered with paint-safe lubricants and high-emulsion cleaning agents, Vijeta Car Wash encapsulates abrasive road dust, mud, and road film so they rinse freely away without scratching vehicle clear coats.

Verified Product Attributes

  • ✓ Clear-Coat & Ceramic Safe: Preserves existing waxes and coatings
  • ✓ High Lubricity Emulsion: Encapsulates road grime to prevent swirls
  • ✓ Streak-Free Rinsing: Dries cleanly without leaving calcified water spots
  • ✓ Multipurpose: Suitable for cars, motorbikes, buses, and transport fleets
Application Standards

Two-Bucket Technique for Best Results

1. Initial Hose-Down

Rinse vehicle thoroughly with cool water from top to bottom to dislodge surface dirt before touching the paintwork with your wash mitt.

2. Suds & Wipe Down

Mix 50 ml of Vijeta Car Wash in a 10-Litre bucket. Submerge your microfiber mitt, apply gently in straight lines, and rinse frequently in clean water.
