Formulation Care

Designed for Frequent, Daily Handwashing

Gentle on the skin, effective against routine everyday grime, and packed in an economical bulk canister for easy dispenser refilling.

Dispenser Refill Economy

The 5-Litre capacity is ideal for refilling wall-mounted or desktop liquid soap dispensers. Suitable for restaurants, commercial kitchens, manufacturing facilities, school restrooms, and residential large households.

Recommended Usage Instructions

Pour a small amount (2-3 ml) onto wet hands. Rub thoroughly to build a rich foaming lather covering palms, back of hands, fingers, and nails for 20 seconds. Rinse cleanly with tap water and dry.
