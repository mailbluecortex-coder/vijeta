What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for wholesale supply?

MOQs depend on the product category and container size. For standard 5 L cans, trial trade orders generally start at 10 to 20 containers. Contact our sales desk at 9423066050 for customized commercial dispatch lots.

Can we mix multiple products in a single dispatch?

Yes. Commercial buyers frequently bundle Handwash, Floor Cleaner, and Dishwash Liquid to optimize local transport and logistics costs.
