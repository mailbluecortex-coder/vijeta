## 1. Design Vision

Create a premium, product-led website for **VIJETA ENTERPRISES** that
feels like a real Indian FMCG and cleaning-products brand. The visual
identity must be drawn from the supplied handwash promotional image:
vibrant pink, white, deep navy typography, floral freshness, water,
cleanliness, and the Marathi brand identity.

The website should communicate:

-   Cleanliness and freshness.
-   Everyday hygiene and care.
-   Product availability and product discovery.
-   A trustworthy local business identity.
-   Easy enquiries for retail, wholesale, and bulk requirements.

The design must not look like a generic AI-generated landing page, a
pre-built corporate template, or a discount-only promotional poster. Use
the uploaded image as a **brand reference**, not as a layout to copy
across every page.

### Design keywords

**Fresh · Confident · Clean · Product-first · Indian FMCG · Premium ·
Approachable**

### 2.4 Typography

Use fonts that render both English and Devanagari correctly.

Suggested pairing: - **English headings:** Manrope or Plus Jakarta
Sans. - **English body:** Inter. - **Marathi / Devanagari:** Noto Sans
Devanagari.

Typography rules: - Use a clear hierarchy with compact headings and
comfortable body line-height. - Avoid overly thin weights for Marathi
text. - Use medium/semibold weights for navigation, labels, and product
names. - Keep long paragraphs readable; do not use decorative fonts for
body copy. - Load only the required font weights to maintain
performance.

## 3. Overall Website Look and Feel

### 3.1 Layout principles

-   Product photography should be the visual focus.
-   Use generous white space and clear section separation.
-   Keep content widths controlled; avoid excessively wide text lines.
-   Use rounded corners consistently, but avoid making every element a
    pill.
-   Use soft, realistic shadows rather than strong neon glows.
-   Use thin, low-contrast borders.
-   Keep navigation simple and predictable.
-   Use real product photos wherever available.
-   Use subtle motion to support product storytelling, not distract from
    it.

### 3.2 Suggested design dimensions

  Element                Recommendation

  Main content width     1180--1280 px

These are starting design values. Adjust after testing with the actual
logo and product photography.

## 14. Motion, Scroll and 3D Interaction

Use motion sparingly to make the product presentation memorable.

### Recommended effects

-   Hero product: subtle scroll-linked parallax or slow 3D-style
    movement.
-   Product showcase: gentle image scale/position transition as the
    section enters view.
-   Product cards: small hover lift and image zoom.
-   Section reveal: restrained fade/translate animation.
-   Navigation: smooth anchor scrolling where appropriate.
-   Buttons: short colour/position transition.

### Optional 3D handwash hero

If a true 3D asset is supplied or created: - Display a GLB/GLTF model of
the actual handwash container. - Allow slow rotation or scroll-linked
rotation. - Keep the model's label, cap, handle, proportions, and
packaging faithful to the real product. - Provide a static optimized
image fallback. - Respect reduced-motion preferences. - Do not block the
page from loading while the 3D model downloads.

If no 3D model is available, use a high-quality transparent product
photograph with layered depth and parallax. Do not pretend a flat image
is an interactive 3D model.

### Motion restrictions

-   No continuous distracting movement.
-   No cursor-following effects that make mobile unusable.
-   No heavy animation before the main content appears.
-   No scroll hijacking.
-   Keep text readable and CTA buttons accessible.
-   Support `prefers-reduced-motion`.

-   Spacious two-column hero.
-   Product grid: 3 or 4 columns depending on content width.
-   Full navigation.
-   Wide product detail gallery and information layout.

### Tablet

-   Two-column hero where space allows.
-   Product grid: 2 columns.
-   Navigation may collapse at the breakpoint where it becomes crowded.
-   Forms remain comfortably readable.

### Mobile

-   Single-column hero with product image prominent.
-   Product grid: 1 or 2 columns depending on device width.
-   Large touch targets.
-   No horizontal scrolling.
-   Forms use full width.
-   Sticky bottom enquiry/WhatsApp action may be used only if it does
    not cover content or browser controls.
-   Keep Marathi and English text wrapping natural.

-   Use transparent PNG/WebP for isolated product pack shots.
-   Use descriptive file names such as `vijeta-handwash-5l-pink.webp`.
-   Add meaningful ALT text.
-   Lazy-load below-the-fold imagery.

-   Do not use the supplied promotional poster as the only product image

-   Do not crop out required product label information or alter the
    product packaging.

## 17. UI Component Specification

Create reusable components for: - Header and mobile navigation. -
Footer. - Primary, secondary, and text buttons. - Product card. -
Product gallery. - Breadcrumbs. - Enquiry form fields. - WhatsApp
action. - Section heading. - Status badge. - Admin sidebar and top

Component styles must use shared design tokens so the colour, spacing,
radius, and typography can be updated consistently.

## 18. Accessibility and Usability

-   Maintain readable text/background contrast.
-   Do not communicate status using colour alone.
-   Provide visible keyboard focus states.
-   Associate every form input with a label.
-   Provide useful validation messages.
-   Give images meaningful ALT text; decorative images should have empty
    ALT.
-   Ensure buttons and links have descriptive names.
-   Respect reduced-motion preferences.
-   Ensure Marathi text is legible on all supported devices.

## 19. Design Do-Not-Do List

-   Do not turn every section pink.
-   Do not copy the promotional poster layout as the entire website.
-   Do not use fake reviews, fake customer logos, fake statistics, or
    fake awards.
-   Do not invent product ingredients, fragrance options, pack sizes,
    prices, or certifications.
-   Do not publish the ₹300 offer without current confirmation and
    terms.
-   Do not redraw or distort the supplied logo.
-   Do not use generic AI-looking gradients, random blobs, or excessive
    glassmorphism.
-   Do not use unrelated stock images that misrepresent the actual
    products.
-   Do not add e-commerce checkout unless separately approved.
-   Do not hardcode products, enquiry counts, phone numbers, or WhatsApp
    numbers in multiple places.
