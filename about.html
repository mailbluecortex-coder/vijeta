Our Story

Serving Daily Hygiene Needs Across Maharashtra

VIJETA ENTERPRISES (VIJETA ENTERPRISES) was established with a singular, clear mandate: to provide high-quality, dependable cleaning liquids that make everyday sanitization accessible, effective, and refreshing.

Operating from Kurduwadi, District Solapur, our company focuses on practical formulations designed for real everyday use. We manufacture essential FMCG hygiene products including Handwash, Floor Cleaner, Dishwash Liquid, Room Freshener, and specialized Automotive Care liquids.

"We do not cut corners on raw material purity or packaging resilience. Our products are formulated to work reliably batch after batch."
